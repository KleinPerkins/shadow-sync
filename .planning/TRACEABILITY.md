## Execution and change rules

The accepted parent plan requires every implementation PR to identify active requirements, design/specification and plan versions, exact source, positive and failure-path verification, independent review and its completion gate. Material changes use a doc-warden successor before dependent implementation. Semantic and structural documentation changes use validated previews and operation-bound apply receipts. Reconciliation precedes phase closeout.

This navigation refresh does not close source export, full application CI/sharding, ordinary reviewer-App delivery, signing, installed safety deployment, migration/update, compatibility retirement or UAT. Their distinct evidence gates remain open where recorded in STATE.md.
